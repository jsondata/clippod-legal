ClipPod Support

For help with an import or export, email jsondata@163.com. Include your iOS version, the output format and any error shown.

Export audio

Choose a video from Photo Library or Files, then select Audio. Drag the waveform handles, choose MP3, M4A or WAV, and tap Export audio. Choose Save to Files or a destination in the share sheet.

If a preview is silent, check that the original video has sound, turn up media volume and check the current audio output.

Make a GIF

Choose GIF and select up to 30 seconds. Adjust the speed, frame rate and size target, then tap Export GIF. If the result cannot fit, shorten the selection or increase the size target.
