ClipPod Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Videos you choose

You choose a video using Photos or Files. ClipPod makes a temporary copy on your iPhone to draw the waveform, preview your selection and create an audio file or GIF. These steps run on your device. ClipPod does not send your media to the developer or use advertising, analytics or tracking services. You can use it without an account.

Saving and sharing

You choose where an export goes in the iOS share sheet. If you select a messaging app or a cloud folder, that service handles the file according to its own privacy policy. A cloud provider may also download a video when you select it for import.

File storage

Working copies are stored in the app’s temporary directory, which iOS can clear. Deleting the app removes its local working copies. You can delete a saved or shared export in the app or service where you put it.

Reading this policy online

The copy in About is available offline. The online copy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
